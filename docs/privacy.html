FileXp

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

FileXp ("FileXP - File Explorer" in the Microsoft Store) is a file explorer for Windows published by Utopia Softech Inc. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

FileXp does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. Everything it does happens on your own PC.

Information on your PC

  • Files and folders. FileXp reads your files and folders only to show, preview, search and manage them at your request. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Search index. For instant search, FileXp keeps a list of file and folder names, sizes and dates from your drives in a file on your PC (%LocalAppData%\FileXp\index.bin). It never leaves your PC. You can turn the index off or rebuild it in Settings, and uninstalling FileXp deletes it.
  • Settings. Your preferences, pinned folders and colour tags are saved on your PC (%AppData%\FileXp\settings.json, or next to the app in the portable version).

Network access

FileXp itself does not send data over the internet. It only opens files and folders you choose, which can include network shares you already have access to. Links you open from FileXp (for example to this website) open in your web browser and are covered by that site's policy.

Other apps

If 7-Zip is installed, FileXp runs it on your PC to open and create archives. FileXp also uses built-in Windows features (the shell, thumbnails and the Recycle Bin), which are covered by Microsoft's privacy statement.

Website and downloads

This website and the downloads are hosted by GitHub. GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address when you visit or download; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Apps installed from the Microsoft Store are subject to Microsoft's privacy statement.

Children

FileXp does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
